23. ADJUSTMENTS
============================================================

Adjustments reconcile system quantity with physical quantity.

Example:

System:
80 kg

Physical:
77 kg

Adjustment:
-3 kg

Fields:

Product
Warehouse
Location
System Quantity
Physical Count
Difference
Reason

Reasons:

Damaged
Lost
Counting Error
Found Stock
Other

Rules:

Warehouse Staff can create.

Inventory Manager validates.

Adjustment cannot result in:

on_hand < reserved

Adjustment must create a ledger entry.

No direct stock editing.

============================================================
